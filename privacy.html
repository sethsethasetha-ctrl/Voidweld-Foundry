Privacy
Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Our approach

Voidweld Foundry is designed to work offline. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The game contains no advertising, analytics, accounts, in-app purchases, tracking, or third-party data collection SDKs.

On-device data

The game stores your level progress, best results, unlocked content, tutorial state, and sound, music, reduced-motion, and high-contrast preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the game and is not sent to us.

Children's privacy

Because we do not collect personal information or provide online features, the game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and retention

We receive no gameplay or personal data, so we have no data to share, retain on our servers, or delete. You can remove locally stored game data by deleting the app from your device, subject to your device's backup settings.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the applicable App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the change takes effect.
